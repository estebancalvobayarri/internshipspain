Mobility Organisation Step by Step

How we organise the mobility

We know Erasmus+ and Turing Scheme mobility projects involve coordination, responsibility and administrative workload.
That is why we follow a clear and structured process.

PROCESS

  1. Initial contact
    Needs analysis: number of students, profiles, and dates
  2. Free tailored proposal
    Customised programme aligned with your Erasmus+ project
  3. Project confirmation
    Agreement formalisation and preparation phase
  4. Mobility preparation
  • Selection of host organisations
  • Assignment of in-company tutors
