About us

Erasmus+ and Turing Scheme Mobility Project Management in Spain

We are a company specialised in the management of international educational projects within the Erasmus+ and Turing Scheme framework.

We support Vocational Education and Training (VET) providers from different European countries and UK in organizing Erasmus+ and Turing Scheme mobility projects in Spain, managing all aspects that can be delegated to a support organisation within the Erasmus+ and Turing Scheme Programme.

We streamline the entire process for the sending organisation and provide local support throughout the whole mobility period.

We work on a small-scale, personalised basis, ensuring close support for every project.

We act as a support organisation, facilitating management and ensuring compliance with Erasmus+ and Turing Scheme quality standards.

  • PROGRAMME COORDINATOR IN SPAIN

    My name is Alberto Sabater and I am the Programme Coordinator at Internship Spain.
    For over 15 years, I have worked as a Physical Education teacher, a significant part of that time within Vocational Education and Training (VET) in the field of sport, where I have been directly involved in the organisation and supervision of student work placements.

    Throughout this period, I have supported hundreds of students, gaining in-depth insight into how mobility programmes operate from within, as well as a clear understanding of the real needs of both educational institutions and learners.

    My perspective is grounded in hands-on experience, having been in the same position you are now—as a teacher responsible for organising internships.

    In addition, I bring experience in international mobility programmes, having managed close to 400 participants in programmes in the United States. This has enabled me to develop a practical approach to managing students abroad, handling unforeseen situations, and ensuring continuous monitoring throughout the entire mobility period.

    At Internship Spain, I personally coordinate each project implemented in Spain. This ensures that both the sending institution and the participants benefit from direct, approachable and continuous support throughout the entire mobility experience.

  • OUR EXPERIENCE

    Our team combines solid expertise in both education and international mobility management. Some members have more than 15 years of experience in the educational sector, particularly in VET in sport, supporting hundreds of students during their work placement periods.

    We understand how educational institutions operate internally, we are aware of teachers’ responsibilities, and we fully recognise what it entails to send students abroad.

    We also have a well-established track record in international mobility and cultural exchange programmes, providing us with a global perspective and extensive experience in participant management, support, and safety in international contexts.

  • OUR WORKING APPROACH

    We are not a traditional agency.

    We act as a supporting organisation for educational institutions:

    • We listen carefully to your needs
    • We design tailored solutions
    • We coordinate every phase of the mobility
    • We provide continuous support before, during and after the mobility

  • MORE THAN A MOBILITY EXPERIENCE

    An Erasmus+ and Turing Scheme mobility is not just a work placement abroad.

    It is a comprehensive experience that combines professional learning, personal development and cultural immersion.

    We take care of the full project organisation, including accommodation, logistics, coordination and ongoing support, ensuring a safe, well-structured and high-quality mobility experience.

  • OUR COMMITMENT

    We work with professionalism, constant communication, a close and personalised approach, and strong attention to detail. Our objective is to ensure that every project is implemented with clarity, safety and trust for both the educational institution and the participants.

    Our goal is to become your trusted partner in the organisation of Erasmus+ mobilities.
