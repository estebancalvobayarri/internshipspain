Your Internship in Spain

Why Choose Spain for Your Erasmus+ Mobility Traineeships

Spain offers one of the most complete environments in Europe for practice-based learning in sport and outdoor education. Your trainees will learn, practise and grow alongside qualified professionals in real professional environments.

What makes Spain different

  • Highly specialised sport and outdoor adventure companies
  • Full integration into real, multicultural professional teams
  • Structured hands-on learning under professional supervision from day one
  • Opportunity to develop Spanish language skills in an authentic setting
  • Safe, quality-compliant host organisations aligned with Erasmus+ standards

Where your trainees will learn and practise

Our partner host organisations are outdoor adventure companies — activity centres where trainees take on the role of sport monitors in practice alongside groups of children and young people.

Trainees always carry out activities under the direct supervision of a qualified instructor. They will assist, learn from and collaborate with experienced monitors while gaining practical skills in activities such as:

  • Hiking and mountain trails
  • Mountain biking routes
  • Rock climbing and abseiling
  • Archery
  • Zip lines and high ropes courses
  • Kayaking and canoeing
  • Paddle surfing and swimming
  • Horse riding excursions
  • Team games, group dynamics and outdoor challenges

This is structured, mentored learning. From their first week, trainees support activities with children, developing leadership, communication and coaching skills — always with a professional by their side to guide and supervise them.

Main destination: Valencia

Valencia and its metropolitan area are among the most attractive destinations in Europe for Erasmus+ mobility — and one of the best cities in the world to live in.

  • One of the best cities in the world to live in — Forbes 2024–2025
  • Top quality of life ranking — InterNations Expat Insider 2025
  • Over 300 days of sunshine per year
  • Active, multicultural city with a strong sports culture
  • Surrounded by nature — mountains, rivers and Mediterranean coast within 30 minutes
  • Real immersion in Spanish and English-speaking professional environments
  • Safe, dynamic and trainee-friendly city

For a VET sport trainee, Valencia is not just a destination — it is a professional springboard.
