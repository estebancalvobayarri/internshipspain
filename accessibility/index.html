ACCESSIBILITY

MOVE ABROAD EXPERIENCE SL is committed to making its website accessible in accordance with Royal Decree 1112/2018, of September 7, on the accessibility of websites and mobile applications of the public sector.

This accessibility statement applies to the website https://internshipspain.com/

COMPLIANCE STATUS

This website is partially compliant with RD 1112/2018 due to the exceptions and lack of compliance of the aspects indicated below.

NON-ACCESSIBLE CONTENT

The content listed below is not accessible for the following reasons:

Non-compliance with RD 1112/2018

There may be some contrast aspects that do not meet the minimum required ratio of 3:1 with respect to adjacent background colors in certain sections of the website. (Requirement number 9.1.4.11 of UNE-EN 301549:2022)

There may be some non-text elements that are not correctly labeled. (Requirement number 9.2.5.3 of UNE-EN 301549:2022).

There may be some texts in another language that are not correctly labeled. (Requirement number 9.3.1.2 of UNE-EN 301549:2022)

There may be occasional code editing errors on some web pages. (Requirement number 9.4.1.1 of UNE-EN 301549:2022)

There may be logos and images with incorrect alternative text, as well as images without textual descriptions (Requirement number 9.1.1.1 of UNE-EN 301549:2022)

There may be links whose function is not correctly identified (Requirement number 9.2.4.4 of UNE-EN 301549:2022)

There may be information or relationships that cannot be programmatically determined or are not available in text form (Requirement number 9.1.3.1 of UNE-EN 301549:2022)

There may be elements implemented through markup languages with incomplete start and/or end tags, duplicate attributes or without a unique identifier (Requirement number 9.4.1.1 of UNE-EN 301549:2022)

Disproportionate burden

Not applicable.

Content outside the scope of applicable legislation

There may be office documents in PDF or other formats published before September 20, 2018 that do not fully comply with all accessibility requirements, although efforts have been made to ensure that most of them do comply.

COMMENTS AND CONTACT INFORMATION

You may submit communications regarding accessibility requirements (article 10.2.a of RD 1112/2018) through the contact form on this website available at the following address: https://internshipspain.com/contact/

For example:

  • Report any possible non-compliance by this website.
  • Communicate other difficulties in accessing the content.
  • Submit any other inquiry or suggestion for improvement related to the accessibility of the website.

You may also submit:

  • A complaint regarding compliance with the requirements of RD 1112/2018
  • A request for accessible information related to:
    • Content excluded from the scope of RD 1112/2018 as established by article 3, section 4.
    • Content exempt from compliance with accessibility requirements because it imposes a disproportionate burden.

In the request for accessible information, the facts, reasons and request must be clearly specified in order to verify that it is a reasonable and legitimate request.

Complaints and claims regarding accessible information must be submitted through the contact form on this website available at the following address: https://internshipspain.com/contact/

OPTIONAL CONTENT

This website is designed for responsive viewing, so it can be displayed correctly on devices such as tablets and mobile devices.

It is also designed to allow text size changes through the menus defined for this purpose according to the browser used.

This can also be done using the following keyboard shortcuts by pressing both keys at the same time:

  • Ctrl + : increase size.
  • Ctrl – : decrease size.
  • Ctrl 0 : restore original size.

Likewise, if a user does not have a mouse, they can navigate through the different elements of the page using the keyboard and the tab key.
