We collaborate with VET providers across Europe to organise Erasmus+ VET and Turing Scheme FE sport internships in Spain, fully aligned with Erasmus+ and Turing Scheme quality standards.

Personalised, agile and fully tailored service. Direct communication and continuous support throughout the mobility.

TARGET GROUP

Designed for Vocational Education and Training (VET) providers (EQF Level 4) wishing to offer their learners the opportunity to complete internships in Spain with full local support.

WHAT THE PROGRAMME INCLUDES

  • Work placement aligned with learners’ profile
  • Accommodation
  • Meals
  • Flight booking and management
  • Airport transfers
  • Local support throughout the stay
  • Assistance with public transport cards (if required)
  • Cultural activities coordination (optional)
  • Guidance on insurance

Erasmus+ and Turing Scheme management and support

  • Continuous coordination with the sending organisation and host company
  • Learning Agreement preparation and signature support
  • Assistance in selecting and assigning in-company tutors
  • Monitoring and periodic evaluation reports
  • Final Europass Mobility documentation management
